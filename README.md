# 崔淑媛房護産廳日記（1693年，韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3619 第1冊） / Choe Sugwonbang hosan-cheong ilgi [Daily Records of the Royal Maternity Directorate for Royal Consort Choe (Sugwon)]: Transcription Dataset

《崔淑媛房護産廳日記》是朝鮮護産廳1693年在漢城所編的漢文日記，記崔淑媛腹痛後的官員召入、醫女與醫官值宿、產室備設、分娩與後續照護。底本為韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3619 冊001，起癸酉九月初三，迄十月十六。可用於研究朝鮮宮廷照護分工與報告制度。

The 1693 Choe Sugwonbang diary records surveillance, deliveries of reports, preparation of a delivery room, birth and follow-up in the Joseon royal court in Hanseong, with reports from medical women and physicians. This is book 1 of Jangseogak shelfmark K2-3619.

依館方11幅定位，正文第002幅左至009幅左，15面、207有字正文欄，另錄三列封面及版面記錄。

The 11 scans cover 15 written body faces and 207 written columns from scan 2 left to scan 9 left, plus three cover entries and page notes.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 16 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。照原稿保存全形空位、重文與圈記，夾注、藏印及館籤分層記錄。原日期採原件書寫，按館方圖號、左右面及欄位定位。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 16 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Original spacings, repetition marks and circles are retained. Small writing, collection stamps and labels are identified separately. Original dates and source image identifiers retain their manuscript context.
